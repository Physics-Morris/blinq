Privacy

Effective October 3, 2026

Blinq does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and it never connects to the internet.

What Blinq checks on your Mac

To decide whether you are using the screen, Blinq reads a few signals from macOS. All of this stays on your Mac and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

  • Time since your last input. Blinq asks macOS how many seconds have passed since the last key press or mouse movement. It never sees which keys you press or where you click.
  • Whether something is keeping the display awake, such as a video player, so that watching counts as screen time.
  • Whether your microphone or camera is in use by any app, so breaks wait until your call ends. Blinq never captures audio, images, or video, and never accesses the content of your microphone or camera.

Blinq does not record your screen, read your files, or track which apps or websites you use.

Settings

Your preferences (break interval, break length, and similar options) are saved locally on your Mac using standard macOS settings storage. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

Blinq collects no information from anyone, including children.
