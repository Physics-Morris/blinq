Blinq

A quiet menu bar timer that reminds you to rest your eyes.

How it works

Blinq lives in your menu bar and counts your screen time while you use your Mac. Every 15 minutes it dims the screen for a short guided eye break: blink, follow a light, look far away, breathe.

  • Counts only while you are present, based on keyboard and mouse activity.
  • Keeps counting while you read or watch a video without touching anything.
  • Resets when you step away, lock your screen, or put the display to sleep.
  • Holds breaks while your microphone or camera is in use, so calls are never interrupted.
  • Do Not Disturb for 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, or until you turn it off.

Support

Questions, bugs, or ideas? Open an issue on GitHub.

Press Esc during a break to snooze it for 5 minutes. To quit, click the eye in the menu bar and choose Quit Blinq.
